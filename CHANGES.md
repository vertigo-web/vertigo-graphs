### Added

* `Graph` component
* `GraphData` - data shared by all chart components
* Building blocks for composed charts: `ChartContainer`, `Grid`, `XAxis`, `YAxis`, `Line`, `Bar`, `FillGradient`,
  `Legend` and `Tooltip`
* `nice_ticks` - round tick values for an axis (steps of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ), for the `divisions`, `value_scale`
  and `decimals` of `YAxisParams` and the `y_divisions` of `GridParams`
